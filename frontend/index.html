Method

What the desk actually does

It is a language classifier, not an investigator. Here is the short version.

I

Intake the copy

You paste the headline and body. Longer text gives the model more pattern to work with.

II

Weigh the words

TF-IDF turns the story into a bag of distinctive terms. Common filler drops away.

III

Draw a margin

LinearSVC places the story on a line between previously labeled fake and true writing.

Limits

A useful warning. Not a verdict of record.

NewsGuard Desk was trained on labeled news text. It notices cadence, vocabulary, and structure. It cannot confirm a quote, a photo, a chart, or a live event.

Treat a Suspect flag as a reason to slow down. Treat a Credible flag as “this writes like reporting,” not “this is proven.”

  • Does Compare writing to labeled fake/true news
  • Does not Search the web or call a fact-checker
  • Does not Judge images, video, or speaker identity
  • Never Use this as legal, medical, or political proof
